Realised profit matches each sale against your oldest purchases and finds first, using sale proceeds after fees. Units sold with no purchase on record (e.g. bought before your saved log history) aren't counted in realised profit — “Read older log history” on the Settings tab can fill the gap. Best options

    This week

    Gym energy comes from your gym training logs. Attacks are counted at 25 energy each from your attack stats; other energy uses aren't reported by the API. Attacks, boosters and refills compare your stats now with Torn's daily snapshot for the week's start day, so a week that doesn't start at 00:00 TCT can be out by part of a day. All times are Torn City Time (UTC).

    A jump is a burst of gym training with Ecstasy or a big happy item (erotic DVD etc.) used within the hour before it; everything you used since your previous training — the stacked Xanax, cans and so on, up to 3 days back — counts towards it. Consumables are costed at what they cost you, oldest stock used first: bought units at the price you paid, and units from crimes, finds, gifts and faction payouts at $0 (the free sources are set on the Settings tab). Anything with no purchase or find on record is valued at today's market price and marked ≈. Points refills cost points rather than cash, so they aren't included. Item use logs come from Settings → Item use log types. Weeks follow the start day set on the Energy page.

    Targets are everyone you've beaten (attacked, mugged, hospitalised, looted, arrested or claimed a bounty on), from your own attack history. Each status check is one API call per player, spaced a second apart; “Check statuses” looks up the most recently beaten targets in the current level and date filters whose status is over a minute old. Hospital and jail timers count down from the last check; once one runs out the target shows as Ready, but they may have been hit again since — check again before attacking. Last active and Ready only filter targets that have been checked. Respect and FF (the fair fight bonus) are from your last win on each target, and the sort options use those. Mugged money comes from your mug logs (Settings → Mug log types) and is counted for mugs in the “Beaten in” range; a mug log that doesn't name the player is matched to your Mugged attack that ended at the same time.

    All hits this week

    Every hit by every faction member from Sun 25 Oct 14:00 TCT to midnight at the end of Sat 31 Oct, in a chain or not, from the faction attack log. These don't change the competition scores, which count chain hits.

    Teams

    Team members are matched to the faction by name. Anyone the announcement names differently from Torn can be put on their team here; changes are kept on this device only.

    MemberPositionTeam

    Scores come from Torn's chain reports for chains running since the start: a hit is any chain attack that left, mugged or hospitalised the target (assists, losses, escapes and stalemates don't count). Team score = team hits ÷ team members; teams level on score share a place. Members need 30+ hits for their team payout. Soul Collector respect and respect per hit leave out bonus hits (10, 25, 50, 100…) and their respect. Prizes halve if a chain ends before reaching 2,500. Raffles and the Pumpkin King / Queen vote are drawn by leadership; this page only shows who's eligible. The chain and its report are re-read every minute while this page is open (2 API calls), plus 1 for new attacks when the key has faction API access. All hits counts every attack a member won (attacked, mugged, hospitalised, arrested, looted or claimed a bounty), in a chain or not.

    Free item sources ($0 cost)

    Items from these logs — crimes, city and dump finds, attack loot, rewards, and items given to you — count as costing nothing. Torn doesn't publish a list of these, so they're picked by name; untick anything that isn't an item find.

    Load once to see available log types.
    Sale log types

    Sales are matched against your oldest purchases first to work out realised profit on the Trading tab. Where a log doesn't state a fee, item market sales are charged the fee above.

    Load once to see available log types.
    Trade log types

    Direct trades with other players. Items you give count as sold for the money you get, and items you get as bought for the money you pay. When a trade has several items, the money is split between them by Torn market value.

    Load once to see available log types.
    Item use log types

    Items you use (Xanax, Ecstasy, energy drinks, erotic DVDs, candy…) come out of stock here and are costed on the Training cost page.

    Load once to see available log types.
